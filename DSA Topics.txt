### 💡 Greedy Algorithms: Key Concepts

- Greedy Choice Property: Making the locally optimal choice leads to a globally optimal solution.
- Feasible Solutions: A greedy approach may or may not always give the globally optimal solution, but it's efficient for many practical problems.
- Greedy vs Dynamic Programming: Unlike DP, greedy algorithms don't require storing previous solutions (no overlapping subproblems).



### 🎯 Why Greedy is Essential:
- It's used in optimization problems like scheduling, resource allocation, and graph algorithms.
- Major tech companies like Google, Amazon, Microsoft, and Facebook love greedy algorithms because they provide efficient solutions with reduced time complexity.
- Greedy algorithms are crucial for competitive coding and also appear frequently in coding assessments.

### 🧠 Key Concepts You’ll Master in Graphs

- DFS & BFS Traversal: Fundamental traversal methods that are used in many problems.
- Shortest Path Algorithms: Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford, and Floyd-Warshall.
- MST (Minimum Spanning Tree): Prim’s and Kruskal’s algorithms for optimizing connections in graphs.
- Topological Sorting: Ordering tasks with dependencies.
- Graph Representation: Adjacency list/matrix, edge list.
- Cycle Detection: In both directed and undirected graphs.
- Connectivity: Connected components, strongly connected components, bridges, and articulation points.



### 🎯 Why Graph Algorithms Are So Crucial
- Graphs are central in real-world applications like network routing, social media analysis, and pathfinding in games (e.g., finding shortest paths in a city).
- They help model complex relationships between entities and can represent web pages, social connections, and more.
- Major tech companies like Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and Facebook often ask graph-related problems to test the depth of your understanding.
